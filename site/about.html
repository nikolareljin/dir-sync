Dir Sync is part of a group of practical, local-first system tools: software that runs on your own machine, does one job clearly, and makes its behavior inspectable.

Dir Sync

A desktop companion for previewing, scheduling, and running one-way directory backups to local folders, network locations, and removable drives. It is released under the MIT License.
